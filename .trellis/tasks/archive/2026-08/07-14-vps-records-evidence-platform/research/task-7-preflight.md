## Capacity and quota contract

- Evidence capacity is independent of attachments. Default project quota is `10 GiB`; warning begins at `80%`; both are explicit validated policy inputs so an administrator/composition layer can adjust them without changing evidence semantics. Attachment quota/readiness must never be queried by an evidence preview, save, capacity pass, or janitor pass.
- Usage is the sum of logical snapshot bytes for the actor/record project. Preview computes a projected outcome using current logical usage plus the preview's estimated canonical bytes and persists that exact outcome in the intent. A usage/read failure produces `QuotaUnavailable`; projected usage over the limit produces `QuotaExceeded`. Neither state may persist a confirmable preview intent.
- Final save must re-evaluate capacity for every new capture after recapture and before any logical snapshot is committed. The revision transaction must enforce the project total atomically enough that concurrent new revisions cannot both pass and exceed the quota. A stale preview that no longer fits fails closed and rolls back the revision/intent consumption. Do not silently drop confirmed intents.
- A text-only revision, reference removal, and continued reuse of an existing logical snapshot remain available when quota is warning/exceeded. Only new logical snapshots consume new capacity. Capacity warning is not a capture denial; exceeded/unavailable are denials. No payload-deduplication shortcut or physical compressed-byte accounting may weaken logical quota.
- Capacity reports must distinguish logical snapshot bytes from physical payload/compressed bytes and orphan payload bytes/counts. Negative values, overflow, unknown project, impossible `used > limit` normalization, or an inconsistent store result fail closed rather than being clamped into a healthy state.
